# Roadmap

## Phase 1 — Foundation

### Delivered

- canonical ecosystem registry;
- governed public directory;
- brand governance;
- platform health automation;
- campaign issue engine;
- launch and distribution playbooks.

## Phase 2A — Campaign Intelligence

### Delivered

- trusted first-party intelligence source registry;
- resilient JSON and GitHub release ingestion;
- normalized campaign-signal schema;
- deterministic six-dimensional opportunity scoring;
- evidence and freshness hard gates;
- SHA-256 signal fingerprinting and issue deduplication;
- capped analyst-review opportunity creation;
- twice-daily Campaign Intelligence Engine workflow;
- CI validation for intelligence policy and source governance.

### Remaining

- ingest additional approved product and Academy/TrustX release signals.

## Phase 2B — Growth Intelligence & Attribution

### Delivered

- deterministic campaign IDs;
- canonical destination enforcement;
- governed per-channel UTM generation;
- campaign lifecycle state machine;
- aggregate-only public-performance contract;
- privacy and sensitive-field rejection;
- funnel KPI calculations;
- policy-controlled 0–100 performance index;
- channel-level scorecards;
- evidence-bounded recommendations;
- idempotent weekly campaign issue generation;
- public-approved scorecard workflow;
- CI validation for growth policy and taxonomy.

## Phase 2C — Private Analytics & SEO Opportunity Intelligence

### Delivered

- private-only first-party analytics runtime boundary;
- gitignored private telemetry/output paths;
- normalized aggregate funnel analytics contract;
- normalized aggregate cross-platform assist contract;
- canonical property-host/platform enforcement;
- person-level identifier and credential-field rejection;
- impossible funnel relationship validation;
- provider allowlist with API-backed providers disabled by default;
- private aggregate analytics scorecards;
- governed SEO policy and topic registry;
- evidence-aware SEO opportunity scoring;
- confidence separation between internal planning and verified external evidence;
- explicit prohibition on invented search-volume/ranking claims;
- internal-link recommendation mapping across ecosystem platforms;
- scheduled public-safe SEO Opportunity Intelligence workflow;
- CI validation for analytics and SEO contracts.

### Next

- provision an isolated private analytics runner;
- connect approved Cloudflare Web Analytics and/or GA4 aggregate adapters;
- connect approved Search Console/Bing/provider evidence normalization;
- executive growth dashboard using reviewed aggregate exports only.

## Phase 2D — Portfolio & Editorial Orchestration

### Delivered

- governed editorial and portfolio policy;
- ISO-week and rolling-window campaign capacity ceilings;
- minimum global campaign spacing;
- same-platform cooldown enforcement;
- per-platform lookback caps;
- coverage-debt prioritization across all governed ecosystem platforms;
- deterministic platform selection with stable tie-breaking;
- platform-specific objective allowlists;
- objective-frequency rotation and repetition caps;
- governed manual workflow overrides without a hidden force bypass;
- public campaign-issue history normalization;
- clean no-op behavior when capacity or eligibility is exhausted;
- machine-readable editorial planning artifact;
- scheduled weekly auto-planning before campaign generation;
- analyst-review issue rationale with portfolio decision metadata;
- CI validation and focused portfolio-planner tests.

### Next

- ingest additional editorial-capacity signals from approved product launch calendars;
- add reviewed executive portfolio coverage reporting;
- connect approved aggregate performance evidence to future prioritization without introducing causal claims.

## Phase 3 — Controlled Distribution

## Phase 4 — Growth Optimization

- executive growth scorecard/dashboard;
