# Measurement Framework

## North-star outcome
Qualified ecosystem demand: meaningful visits, product exploration, trials/inquiries, enterprise conversations, toolkit purchases, academy enrollment intent and repeat engagement.

## KPI hierarchy

### Awareness
- qualified impressions;
- branded search growth;
- earned mentions and citations;
- share of relevant technical conversations.

### Engagement
- click-through rate;
- engaged sessions;
- report/tool/course depth;
- repeat visitors;
- content saves/shares by target audiences.

### Commercial intent
- contact/enterprise CTA clicks;
- demo or assessment inquiries;
- API/toolkit interest;
- academy enrollment intent;
- qualified lead creation.

### Ecosystem expansion
- cross-platform navigation rate;
- percentage of users touching two or more ecosystem products;
- conversion assisted by another CYBERDUDEBIVASH® property.

## Measurement rule
Do not optimize campaigns exclusively for raw impressions. Prefer qualified engagement, authority, referral quality and commercial movement.
