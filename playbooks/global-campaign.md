# Global Campaign Playbook

1. Select one business objective: authority, demand, adoption, launch, education or trust.
2. Choose one primary audience and at most two secondary audiences.
3. Build one evidence-backed core narrative.
4. Create a flagship asset on an owned CYBERDUDEBIVASH® property.
5. Adapt into channel-native derivatives instead of posting identical copy everywhere.
6. Use canonical URLs and campaign parameters consistently.
7. Cross-link only the most relevant ecosystem products.
8. Review security, accuracy and brand gates.
9. Publish in controlled waves.
10. Measure after 24h, 72h and 7d; record learning and next action.
