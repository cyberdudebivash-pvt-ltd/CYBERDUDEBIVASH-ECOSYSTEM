# Platform / Feature Launch Playbook

## Pre-launch
- verify production availability;
- capture approved screenshots/demo footage;
- freeze canonical product description and CTA;
- validate SEO metadata, OG image and social preview;
- prepare launch brief and FAQ;
- identify measurable launch target.

## Launch
- publish owned flagship announcement;
- distribute executive, technical and commercial variants;
- link directly to the canonical product page;
- monitor errors, broken links and audience questions.

## Post-launch
- publish technical proof/use case;
- answer objections with evidence;
- convert questions into FAQ/content backlog;
- review qualified traffic, leads and cross-platform movement.
